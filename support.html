TyunReTso Support

TyunReTso is an Armenian keyboard for iPhone and iPad with the standard phonetic layout, Western Armenian and alphabetical layouts, swipe gestures, word suggestions, an emoji page and proper Armenian punctuation.

Turning the keyboard on

  1. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards.
  2. Tap Add New Keyboard… and choose TyunReTso.
  3. In any app, hold the globe key and pick TyunReTso.

Vibration does not work

iOS allows a keyboard to vibrate only when Full Access is on. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → TyunReTso and turn on Allow Full Access. The keyboard never sends anything anywhere, with or without it.

Restoring Pro on a new device

Open the TyunReTso app, go to Settings, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchase. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.

The 14-day trial

The trial is free and simply ends after 14 days. Nothing is charged automatically. Afterwards the free keyboard keeps working, and Pro can be unlocked for one payment at any time.
