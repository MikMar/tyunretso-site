TyunReTso Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

TyunReTso is an Armenian keyboard for iPhone and iPad. It is built so that it has nothing to collect.

What the keyboard does with what you type

Everything you type stays on your device. The keyboard contains no networking code, never connects to the internet, and never sends keystrokes, text, or usage data anywhere, to us or to anyone else.

Full Access

iOS lets you grant a keyboard "Full Access". TyunReTso works without it. The only feature that needs it is haptic feedback (vibration on key press), because iOS allows a keyboard to vibrate only when Full Access is on. Granting it does not change what the keyboard does with your text: nothing leaves your device either way.

Data stored on your device

  • Your settings (layout, sounds, gestures and similar).
  • A personal dictionary of words you type that the built-in dictionary does not know, used only to offer suggestions. You can view, edit and delete it in the app at any time.
  • Recently used emoji.

This data lives in the app's private storage on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

The optional Pro unlock and its free trial are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details and no personal information about buyers.

Analytics, advertising, accounts

None. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no accounts.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Кратко по-русски

TyunReTso не собирает никаких данных. Всё, что вы печатаете, остаётся на вашем устройстве: у клавиатуры нет доступа к интернету, и она ничего никуда не отправляет. Полный доступ необязателен и включает только вибрацию. Настройки, личный словарь и недавние эмодзи хранятся только на устройстве и удаляются вместе с приложением. Покупки обрабатывает Apple; мы не получаем платёжных данных.
